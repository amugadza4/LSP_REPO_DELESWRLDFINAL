# Question 4 – Reflection on AI in Programming

## Answer

My perspective on how AI is used in programming has changed drastically. I 
have always known AI was capable, but having the ability to use it freely 
without having to hide it is different. Over the years I have used AI, but 
it was during times when I was not supposed to. I was always scared to use 
it but did anyway.

I still get nervous because I feel like I need to know more than AI, but I 
plan to use AI in the future as I step into my career in tech. It is very 
helpful and fast-paced for tasks that could take a while. I am glad to be 
learning how to utilize it to the best of my ability.
